Chanyoung Park 박찬영

Associate Professor, KAIST

Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering · Graduate School of AI · Graduate School of Data Science · Department of AX
Director, Data Science & Artificial Intelligence Lab (DSAIL)

I work on representation learning for graphs, molecules, and multimodal data, and on LLM agents that reason and act — increasingly in the service of scientific discovery. Before KAIST, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and received my Ph.D. from POSTECH.

Prospective students

I am actively seeking passionate and self-motivated Ph.D. students, M.S. students, and postdocs. Undergraduate internships are open every summer and winter, and an internship is the best way to prepare for applying as a graduate student. If you are interested, send me an email with your interests, CV, and transcript.

You can join the lab through the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, the Graduate School of Data Science, the Graduate School of AI, or the Department of AX (College of AI).
