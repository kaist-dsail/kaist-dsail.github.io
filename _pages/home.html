From representation learning to reasoning and autonomous discovery.

We are DSAIL at KAIST, led by Prof. Chanyoung Park. We develop AI systems that learn from complex relational, multimodal, and scientific data, and extend these representations toward personalization, reasoning, agentic decision making, and scientific discovery.

Explore our researchJoin us

Research at a glance

Five areas, described in full on the Research page.

AI for Science & Engineering

Representations and agents for molecules, materials, cells, physical systems, and circuits — from property prediction to synthesis planning, with LLM agents that read spectra, plan experiments, and run parts of the scientific loop.

Read more →

Agentic AI & Reasoning

LLM agents that reason over multiple steps, use tools and memory, act, and improve from feedback — evaluated on the process, not just the final answer, and aligned to each user's safety needs.

Read more →

Graph & Relational Learning

Learning from relational data — networks, hypergraphs, and interaction structures — with representations that generalize under noise, scarce labels, and distribution shift.

Read more →

Personalized Intelligence & Recommendation

Modeling users and items from interaction data, from collaborative filtering to LLM-based and self-evolving recommender systems.

Read more →

Multimodal Intelligence

Aligning images, video, and language into representations that support fine-grained understanding and compositional reasoning, efficiently.

Read more →
